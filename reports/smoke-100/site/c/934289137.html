Brief

TROPER AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Oslo, founded 2024.[31][20][36][32] Registered industry: Utgivelse av annen programvare (NACE 58.290).[29] Its website describes it as: “Troper er et spesialutviklet rapportskrivingsverktøy for deg som jobber med tilstandsrapporter, arealrapporter eller skaderapporter.”[13] Latest filed accounts (2025): revenue NOK 176 k, operating result -NOK 864, net result -NOK 864, equity NOK 99 k.[35][28][16][10][27][6][12] Leadership: CEO Bjørn Fosser and chair Simen Wilberg Nilsen.[25][11] 1 registered workplace(s) (subunits).[18] Verified website: https://www.troper.no/.[19]
