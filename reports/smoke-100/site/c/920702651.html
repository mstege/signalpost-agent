Brief

VOLVER STUDIOS AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Oslo, founded 2018.[21][37][46][32] Registered industry: Industri-, produkt- og motedesignvirksomhet (NACE 74.110).[17] Its website describes it as: “A Norwegian company called Norwegian Volver makes traditional Norwegian carpets and rugs for both residential and commercial spaces.”[28] It presents itself publicly as “Volver Studio Norway”.[42] Latest filed accounts (2025): revenue NOK 3.0 m (-21% vs 2024), operating result NOK 53 k, net result NOK 36 k, equity -NOK 95 k.[43][2][38][15][27][52][25][11] Equity was negative at the end of 2025.[52] Leadership: CEO Ksenia Stanishevski and chair Ksenia Stanishevski.[39][14] 1 registered workplace(s) (subunits).[8] Verified website: https://volverstudios.com/, linking 3 company-owned profile(s) (instagram, pinterest, tiktok).[53][49][19][16] Most recent company news (2026-08-18): “Vollsveien 2A Office Social Zones”.[29]
