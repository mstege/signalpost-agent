Brief

MR. NORWAY AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Oslo, founded 2013.[14][34][54][6] Registered industry: Agenturhandel med tekstiler, klær, pelsskinn, skotøy og lærvarer (NACE 46.160).[46] Its website describes it as: “Mr. Norway AS and its owner Tom Haugvik, is a fashion agent/distributor located in Oslo. We represent international contemporary brands in the mid segment.”[16] Latest filed accounts (2025): revenue NOK 170 k (+3% vs 2024), operating result NOK 38 k, net result NOK 39 k, equity -NOK 1.1 m.[47][37][51][11][3][26][31][24] Equity was negative at the end of 2025.[26] Leadership: CEO Tom Haugvik and chair Tom Haugvik.[19][2] 1 registered workplace(s) (subunits).[17] Verified website: https://mrnorway.no/, linking 1 company-owned profile(s) (instagram).[21][23]
