Brief

SLETTÅS AS is a private limited company (AS) based in Orkland, founded 2015.[18][37][46][7] Registered industry: Engroshandel med parfyme og kosmetikk (NACE 46.450).[32] Its website describes it as: “Slettås VA Teknikk jobber innenfor vann- og avløpsteknikk, farlig avfall og tørrsuging i Trøndelag. Vi har flere avdelinger i Norge”[22] It presents itself publicly as “Slettås VA Teknikk”.[51] Latest filed accounts (2025): revenue NOK 158 k (-71% vs 2024), operating result -NOK 129 k, net result -NOK 296 k, equity -NOK 415 k.[21][50][14][53][56][74][20][49] Equity was negative at the end of 2025.[74] Leadership: CEO Siri Sprauten Slettås and chair Frank Slettås.[30][77] 1 registered workplace(s) (subunits).[62] Verified website: https://slettaas.com/, linking 3 company-owned profile(s) (facebook, instagram, youtube).[52][78][47][34] Most recent company news (2021-07-01): “Slettås Vann- og Avløpsteknikk blir en del av VA365.”.[41]
